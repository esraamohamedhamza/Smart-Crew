# Smart Crew AI ✈️
### Next-Gen Flight Operations & Predictive Crew Management

**Smart Crew** is a high-fidelity AI-driven platform designed to revolutionize aviation management. It integrates real-time data analytics with predictive AI to ensure seamless flight operations, crew safety, and automated conflict resolution.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. AI Conflict Resolution (Auto-Heal)
- **Problem:** Sudden schedule overlaps or crew unavailability.
- **Solution:** Our "Auto-Heal" engine uses AI to instantly re-assign crew members and resolve scheduling conflicts without manual intervention.

### 2. Predictive Risk Analysis
- Real-time monitoring of external factors (Weather, Technical issues) and internal factors (Crew fatigue, Certification expiry).
- Dynamic risk scoring to alert dispatchers before a crisis occurs.

### 3. Integrated Flight History
- Absolute traceability of every operational change.
- High-precision timestamps (HH:MM:SS DD-MM-YYYY) for audit-ready logging.

### 4. Advanced Fleet Visibility
- Interactive Gantt charts and timelines for a bird's-eye view of all ongoing and upcoming flights.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
- **Premium Dark Identity:** Designed for high-stakes operational environments with a focus on visual hierarchy and reduced eye strain.
- **Technical UX:** Optimized dashboard for quick decision-making under pressure.
- **Consistent Sub-Branding:** Featuring "Powered by Smart Crew AI" sub-text to establish trust in the AI's logic.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture
- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript.
- **Routing:** TanStack Router (Type-safe navigation).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Shadcn/UI (Custom Enterprise Theme).
- **Animations:** Framer Motion for smooth state transitions.
- **Data Viz:** Recharts for analytical gauging.
